# Video Analysis — Mivon Creative Agency (mivon.bslthemes.com)

Source: 22-second screen recording of the Mivon homepage, analysed frame by frame.
This clone recreates the look from the frames; no original assets were copied.

## Design tokens
- Background #0E0E0E / #111111, panels #161616 / #1A1A1A, white text, muted #9A9A9A
- Accent lime #D6FF3F (footer logo, one portfolio card), blue #1A2BFF card, red leather card
- Tight grotesque sans, huge headings, 24px card radius, pill buttons/nav

## Sections (in order)
1. **Header** — "mivon" logo left, centred pill nav HOME / PAGES / WORKS / BLOG / CONTACT
   with dropdowns, dark-mode toggle + hamburger right.
2. **Hero** — "Blending creativity & strategy to build brands & design websites."
   (last phrase underlined); pills: Search Engine Optimization / Branding Strategy /
   Product Design / Digital Marketing; right paragraph + KNOW MORE; rotating circular
   text badge with green 3D blob.
3. **Portfolio** — horizontal carousel: Contra Materia, Dexa Technology, Mana Hotels,
   Noise Headphone, Centrica, Sandstone Sim.
4. **About** — "OUR CAPABILITIES." + long paragraph about bold identities.
5. **Services** — Digital Marketing, Product Design, Search Engine Optimization,
   each with a chrome 3D shape and KNOW MORE.
6. **Marquee** — giant outline text "Digital Agency" scrolling infinitely.
7. **Team** — "MEET OUR TEAM." + paragraph, 4 portraits, stats 4.9/5 Client Reviews,
   2k+ Product Launch, 35+ Ready Teammates, "GLOBAL RECOGNITION / We thrive to
   create design that make impact not just impressions."
8. **Approach** — giant "APPROACH" with 5 floating cards: Innovative Creativity,
   Collaborative Partnership, Results-Driven Strategy, Transparent Communication,
   Passion for Impact (one card white).
9. **Testimonial slider** — Carol Buyers, Ceo Founder, Arch Studio, 1/3 counter.
10. **Client logos** — GitHub, Zumar Cons, Mathew Co., Archin + "125+ clients worldwide".
11. **Awards** — year rows 2020–2025 (Featured Design of the Week, Behance Curated
    Work, Agency of the Year, Medium articles).
12. **Blog** — "Latest creative thinking journals." + 3 cards, all dated May 7, 2026.
13. **Footer** — "Let's Start your project" + GET IN TOUCH, lime "mivon" logo,
    pill links, socials, newsletter input, "© COPYRIGHT 2026 BY BSLTHEMES".

## Animations observed
1. Custom grey cursor circle that grows over links/cards.
2. Hero word-by-word staggered reveal; underline draws in.
3. Rotating circular text badge, slowly spinning, with morphing green blob.
4. Portfolio horizontal drag/scroll carousel; card images scale on hover.
5. About paragraph word-by-word opacity reveal tied to scroll.
6. Services cards fade up staggered on scroll; hover lift; floating chrome shapes.
7. Giant outline "Digital Agency" infinite marquee.
8. Team portrait parallax on scroll.
9. APPROACH pinned giant outline text; 5 cards float in staggered around it.
10. Testimonial slider with prev/next + 1/3 counter.
11. Awards rows highlight + arrow on hover.
12. Blog card image zoom on hover.
13. Header shrinks / hides on scroll down; dropdowns animate open.
14. Footer reveals on scroll into view.

## What could NOT be matched exactly
- Original photography / project imagery replaced with Unsplash stock photos and
  CSS gradient panels (same colour blocks: blue, red leather, lime).
- Original 3D chrome renders replaced with CSS gradient shapes (sphere, torus, cone).
- Exact font of the original (a commercial grotesque) replaced with Inter.
- Testimonials #2 and #3 invented in the same style (only #1, Carol Buyers, is from the video).
- Team member names/roles are placeholders — the video did not show them legibly.
